WHY THIS WORKFLOW

不是“给一个提示词，换一套模板”。Not “one prompt, one generic template.”

它把一个账号真正需要保持一致的六件事放进同一条生产线：说给谁听、从哪里得到材料、讲哪个角度、长什么样、什么时候必须停下来确认、最后怎么交付。It puts the six things an account must keep consistent in one production line: audience, source material, angle, visual language, approval gates, and final handoff.

01 SOURCE

真实来源先行

先记事实、链接、解释和限制，再写观点。Log facts, links, interpretation, and limits before writing the point of view.

02 ACCOUNT

账号不是空白画布

先做账号简报，不把一个号的口吻、视觉和受众套给另一个号。Build an account brief first. Do not transplant one account's voice, visuals, or audience onto another.

03 TOPIC

选题是可比较的

一次只给 3 个结构不同的选题卡，让选择发生在制作之前。Present three structurally different topic cards, so the decision happens before production.

04 GATE

创作者掌握闸门

未被明确选中的主题，不会被当作批准去生成图片。An unselected idea is never treated as approval to generate images.

05 ART DIRECTION

视觉可以定制

账号资产、参考账号、风格库三条路线；借方法，不克隆 IP。Three routes—account assets, references, style library. Borrow methods, never clone IP.

06 DELIVERY

交付的是发布包

PNG、九宫格、标题、正文、标签、来源注释与上传顺序。PNGs, contact sheet, title, caption, tags, source note, and upload order.

8 STEPS / 八步工作流

从信息源到发布，不跳步。From source to post, without skipping the important steps.

流程既可以只停在“选题卡”，也可以在你确认后继续完成图文。它不是强迫一次做完所有事，而是控制每一步该做什么、不该越过什么。The workflow can stop at topic cards or continue to a finished carousel after your approval. It does not force every step at once; it controls what each step should—and should not—do.

CUSTOMIZE THE STUDIO

同一个工作流，不会做出同一种账号。One workflow should not produce one kind of account.

使用者可以在五个层面定制内容。它们共同决定“这个账号说什么、为什么能说、怎么呈现、如何持续”。Creators can customize five layers. Together they decide what the account says, why it can say it, how it looks, and how it stays consistent.

账号定位 Account identity

决定选题的边界与表达的口吻。Defines topic boundaries and voice.

  • 受众与读者起点 / audience and starting point
  • 内容支柱 / content pillars
  • 内容目标与 CTA / goal and CTA

来源池 Source pool

决定内容有没有材料、是否值得讨论。Determines whether content has material and is worth discussing.

  • RSS、公众号、链接 / RSS, WeChat articles, links
  • 评论、采访、产品材料 / comments, interviews, product material
  • 可核查的事实 / verifiable observations

主题结构 Topic structure

决定图文不是一堆标题变体。Prevents carousels from becoming headline variations.

  • 机制拆解 / mechanism breakdown
  • 材料型故事 / material-led story
  • 可执行实验 / practical experiment

系列规则 Series rules

决定读者一眼能否认出是你的内容。Helps readers recognize that a post belongs to the account.

  • 栏目名和更新节奏 / series name and cadence
  • 封面共同标识 / repeated cover signature
  • 结尾互动方式 / ending interaction pattern

第一次使用，只需要准备这些。For the first run, prepare only this.

不需要一次提供完整品牌手册。先给足以决定第一篇内容的最小信息，后续再根据发布结果补全账号档案。You do not need a complete brand manual. Provide the minimum needed to decide the first post, then deepen the account profile with real results.

TOPIC SELECTION

不是挑一句标题，是挑一种内容价值。You are not choosing a headline. You are choosing a kind of value.

默认给 3 组结构不同的选题，避免“同一个意思换三个标题”。每一组都必须写清来源、读者收获、叙事方式和不做出的承诺。The default delivers three different structures—not three headlines for the same idea. Each must state the source, reader value, narrative, and what it does not claim.

TYPE 01

机制拆解
Mechanism breakdown

用一个具体作品、产品或现象，回答“它为什么这样有效 / 为什么让人停下来”。Use one product, work, or phenomenon to explain why it works or holds attention.

适合 / Best for：知识、游戏、工具、产品、行业观察
TYPE 02

材料型故事
Material-led story

从一条文章、访谈、评论、数据或旧材料，提炼一个值得被重新看见的观察。Start with an article, interview, comment, data point, or archive and surface an observation worth revisiting.

适合 / Best for：文化、趋势、社群、个人经验、品牌叙事
TYPE 03

可执行实验
Practical experiment

给读者一个能立即尝试、记录、比较或复盘的步骤，而不是只给鼓励。Give readers a step they can try, record, compare, or review now—not just encouragement.

适合 / Best for：教程、挑战、工作流、创作练习
VISUAL ROUTES

视觉不是后期“美化”，是账号识别的一部分。Visual direction is not a finishing touch. It is part of account recognition.

制作前必须选择一种视觉输入；系统把它整理为配色、封面骨架、字体层级、头像/IP 规则和系列重复规则。Before production, choose one visual input. The system turns it into a palette, cover skeleton, type hierarchy, avatar/IP rule, and series repetition rule.

FAQ

先把最重要的边界说清楚。The important boundaries, made explicit.

会自动套用某个账号的风格吗？Will it automatically copy a certain account's style?

不会。必须先给账号资产、参考方向或选择风格库；参考只能转化为方法，不是复制的素材。No. You must provide account assets, reference direction, or a library choice. References are transformed into methods, not copied assets.

能直接出完整图文吗？Can it create a finished carousel directly?

可以，但默认先给选题卡，等你确认；这是为了避免做出不属于这个账号的“完整成品”。Yes, but it starts with topic cards and waits for your approval. This avoids producing polished work that does not belong to the account.

可以只用中文吗？Can I use it in Chinese only?

可以。中文是小红书任务的主要使用语言；页面、教程和提示词也同时提供英文，方便国际协作与 GitHub 搜索。Yes. Chinese is a primary language for Xiaohongshu work; English is included for international collaboration and GitHub discovery.

它会承诺数据或流量吗？Does it promise metrics or traffic?

不会。它提高的是来源可信度、账号一致性、选题决策质量和手机端可读性；真实结果仍需要发布与复盘验证。No. It improves source rigor, account consistency, topic decisions, and mobile readability. Results still need publication and real-world review.
